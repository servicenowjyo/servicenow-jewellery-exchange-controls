## Public retail facts vs assumptions
Emmadi Silver Jewellery public FAQ: https://emmadisilverjewellery.com/pages/faq . The retrieved page describes 925 sterling silver and lifetime exchange at 55% of original purchase value. Its general no-return/no-exchange language coexists with a separately described lifetime-exchange benefit: staff must validate eligibility and current terms. We did not inspect store systems, bills, employees, settlement data or internal controls. No actual delays, deficiencies or savings are asserted. The store-locator page did not establish Tirupati presence during this research: https://emmadisilverjewellery.com/pages/store-locator . All SYN customers, amounts and bill IDs are invented. Eligible purchase base, full-item-only handling, duplicate definition and ₹50,000 review threshold are demo assumptions. No product photos or merchant logo were copied.
